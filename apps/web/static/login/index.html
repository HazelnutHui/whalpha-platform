The intelligence path

One decision system, built in connected layers.

WH Alpha is organized around the questions a trader actually asks. Each layer narrows the next decision while keeping evidence, counterevidence, and uncertainty visible.

Market state→Strength direction→Sector / theme→Stock candidate→Trade preparation→Entry / invalidation→Position management
  1. Live now01

    Market Regime & Opportunities

    Determine whether the market truly supports broad risk-taking through regime, directional strength, ETF relationships, and counterevidence.

    • Primary and Secondary Universe views
    • 5 / 10 / 20-session relationships
  2. Live now02

    Sector ETF Rotation

    Compare fixed sector ETF proxies with SPY across independent 5, 10, and 20-session windows, acceleration, and leadership persistence.

    • Leadership and deterioration without a composite score
    • Themes stay unavailable until membership is governed
  3. Live now03

    Market Structure & Activity

    Explain what changed today through breadth, advancing and declining participation, benchmark structure, and industry proxies.

    • Participation—not claimed capital flow
    • Current state plus historical path
  4. Live now04

    Stock Candidates

    Compare stocks inside distinct strategy channels, then show why each candidate ranks, where it sits, and what would invalidate the setup.

    • Momentum, pullback, continuation, reversal, and defensive channels
    • Ranking reasons before a single total score
  5. Live now05

    Evidence & Decision Context

    Keep raw values, parameters, contributions, supporting evidence, counterevidence, signal age, and recent price path open to inspection.

    • Explainable by design
    • Fail closed when formal data is unavailable
  6. Planned next06

    Watchlist & Trade Preparation

    Turn an interesting stock into a monitored setup with trigger, pullback zone, support, resistance, threshold distance, signal age, and invalidation.

    • Position and timing before action
    • Fewer, more deliberate alerts
  7. Planned07

    Options Expression

    Compare how to express a stock view through calls, puts, debit spreads, covered calls, moneyness, and DTE—without treating stock returns as option returns.

    • Liquidity, spread, OI, IV, Greeks, and event risk
    • Payoff and time-decay trade-offs
  8. Planned08

    Fundamentals, Valuation & Events

    Add point-in-time financials, earnings quality, relative valuation, catalysts, and event context to price-based evidence.

    • No hindsight-contaminated fundamentals
    • No false surprise model without expectations data
  9. Planned later09

    Position Management

    Reassess an open position as evidence changes: thesis health, invalidation, event exposure, concentration, and post-entry risk.

    • Decision support, never automatic execution
    • IBKR-first integration when this phase begins

How WH Alpha thinks

Built for judgment, not obedience.

The platform should make a human decision clearer—not hide uncertainty behind a confident label.

01

Explain before ranking

A high or low rank must reveal the inputs, position, and trade-offs that produced it.

02

Counterevidence is first-class

Every constructive view should show what argues against it and what would make it fail.

03

Context changes meaning

The same setup can mean something different under a different market regime or sector environment.

04

Different instruments, different outcomes

Price and volume are not capital flow, and a stock signal is not an options-return prediction.
