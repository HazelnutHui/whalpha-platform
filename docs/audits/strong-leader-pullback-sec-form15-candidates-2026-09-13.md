## Interpretation boundary

reporting duty. Its filing date, certification date, selected rule, CIK, class
text, and Commission file number do not alone prove listed-security identity,
last tradable date, exchange-delisting effective date, transaction completion,
OTC continuation, consideration, or terminal return. Transaction filings and
corroborating market/lifecycle evidence remain the next bounded stage.
