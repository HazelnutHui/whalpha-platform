# Quant Research Campaign Three Hypothesis Registry V1

## Purpose

`quant-research-campaign-hypothesis-registry/1.0` freezes the outcome-blind
hypothesis-intake and seven-dimensional deduplication result for Campaign
Three. It answers which ideas may proceed to input qualification before any
new Development outcome is read.

This is not the Campaign Three screening protocol. It registers no formal
outcome trial, creates no Ledger V4 entry, and grants no outcome access.

## Deduplication identity

Each proposal is compared across:

1. economic mechanism;
2. information set and cutoff;
3. exact formula and transformation;
4. Universe and eligibility;
5. label and horizon;
6. parameter neighborhood; and
7. related hypothesis family.

Changing a display name does not create a new idea. Exact duplicates stop;
near-duplicates remain recorded but do not consume a prospective trial.

## Frozen intake result

Five proposals are retained. Four may proceed to outcome-blind input
qualification:

- breakout position conditioned on reconstructed breadth;
- dollar-volume activity surprise conditioned on market participation;
- defensive relative resilience conditioned on broad-market weakness; and
- safer-oriented residual volatility conditioned on broad-market volatility,
  as a risk guard rather than candidate Alpha.

The proposed ATR-compression plus broad-trend interaction is rejected before
outcomes because it is too close to the consumed Pullback/ATR-compression
family. This rejected record prevents later rediscovery from silently becoming
a new test.

The prospective finite budget is three candidate-Alpha interactions and one
risk-guard interaction. It does not increase the 14 consumed formal trials
until a later protocol and Ledger V4 are frozen.

## Estimand and interpretation boundary

The intended interaction is session-level modulation of cross-sectional rank
IC: `q(t) = alpha + beta * S(t) + error(t)`. A market-state scalar must not be
multiplied into every security and then cross-sectionally ranked, because a
positive session-constant scalar leaves ranks unchanged and is not a genuine
interaction test.

The primary stock horizon is three sessions from next open. One- and
five-session values are diagnostics only. Price and volume remain market-
behavior proxies, never fund-flow evidence. The market-state artifact is
reconstructed research evidence, not as-operated history.
