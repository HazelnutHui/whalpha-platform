# Campaign Three Input Qualification V1

## Purpose

`quant-research-campaign-input-qualification-report/1.0` decides whether each
accepted Campaign Three hypothesis has enough factor variation, market-state
variation, temporal balance, and exact Development alignment to enter a later
finite screening protocol. It is outcome-blind and does not register a trial.

The protocol is bound to the frozen Campaign Three hypothesis registry, the
exact replayed Factor Catalog V1 diagnostics, the exact replayed Factor Catalog
V2 qualification, and the qualified reusable Market-State artifact.

## Frozen Development intersection

- 106 exact signal sessions from 2025-07-22 through 2026-01-07;
- two equal chronological halves of 53 sessions;
- no Validation, Holdout, Production, or post-Development outcome access; and
- effective-dated reconstructed Membership by stable `instrument_id`, with its
  not-as-operated limitation retained.

## Input gates

Each factor requires at least 80 eligible sessions, 40 in each half, at least
100 available instruments per eligible session, and at least two distinct
cross-sectional values. Source-level same-session tie excess may not exceed
25%.

Each state requires at least 80 available sessions, 40 in each half, at least
20 distinct values overall, and 10 in each half. Candidate-Alpha states also
require at least 20 observations on both sides of the registered natural zero,
at least eight on both sides in each half, no side above 85% of observations,
and no same-side run longer than 35 sessions. The continuous volatility state
for the risk guard is not forced through an economically false zero-side test.

No full-panel quantile may be introduced after seeing the data to rescue a
failed state. A rejected design may be retained as failed research evidence,
but it cannot consume an outcome trial.

## Evidence strategy

The V1 factors use the exact outcome-blind per-session availability report plus
the report's pairwise-eligibility coverage to prove cross-sectional variation.
The V2 factors are replayed only over the 106 Development signal sessions from
the qualification-bound canonical inputs and private split extension. This
bounded replay reads no labels and does not rerun the five-year database.

## Authority boundary

A ready report permits only protocol and Ledger V4 preparation. It does not
authorize Development outcome reads, a screening execution, Validation,
Holdout, Model Construction, Candidate activation, `/data` writes,
publication, deployment, order generation, or trading.
