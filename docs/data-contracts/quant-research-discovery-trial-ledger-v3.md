## Append-only rule

V1 and V2 may not be removed, reordered, relabeled, or overwritten. A later
factor campaign must register its finite trials in a new ledger version before
reading outcomes and must disclose that its design is adaptive to the 14
consumed Development trials. A new factor definition, interaction, horizon, or
gate is a new trial; it is not a correction to this ledger.
