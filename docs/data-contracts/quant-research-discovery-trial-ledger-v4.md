# Quant Research Discovery Trial Ledger V4

## Purpose

`quant-research-discovery-trial-ledger/4.0` is the append-only cumulative state
after Campaign Three protocol registration and before any new Development
outcome is read.

## Registered content

- carries the completed V1 and V2 campaigns and all 14 consumed trials without
  removal, reorder, relabeling, or changed outcomes;
- appends exactly two candidate-Alpha interaction trials and one risk-guard
  interaction trial as `registered_pending_development_screen`;
- records 17 cumulative formal trials: 11 candidate Alpha and 6 risk guard;
- retains two Campaign Three before-outcomes rejections outside the formal
  outcome budget;
- records zero new outcomes, zero new admitted Alpha, and zero model inputs;
  and
- requires a separate typed Development-access grant for one formal execution
  and one exact replay.

## Authority boundary

Ledger registration is accounting, not execution authority. Development,
Validation, Holdout, Model Construction, Strategy Expression, Candidate
activation, publication, broker access, and trading remain closed until their
separate gates are satisfied.
