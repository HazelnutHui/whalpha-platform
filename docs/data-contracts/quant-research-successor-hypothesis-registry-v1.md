# U.S. Successor Hypothesis Registry V1

## Purpose

Freeze a finite, outcome-blind intake after the completed factor-space
diagnostic and before any new source or Development outcome is read.

## Registered cards

| Role | Economic family | Definition | Current gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate Alpha | analyst expectation revision | 21-session point-in-time FY1 EPS consensus revision scaled by aligned price | point-in-time source qualification |
| Candidate Alpha | fundamental quality | as-filed TTM operating cash flow less net income, scaled by average assets | point-in-time source qualification |
| Candidate Alpha | positioning pressure | sign-reversed change in published short interest / shares across two publications | point-in-time source qualification |
| Risk guard | scheduled event risk | known earnings event from next open through the third session close | historical event-calendar qualification |

The registry counts zero formal trials. Daily short volume is not short
interest; current/restated fundamentals are not historical as-filed knowledge;
and a current earnings calendar cannot reconstruct historical announcement
revisions.

## Authority

The contract authorizes only source and implementation qualification. It does
not authorize Development outcomes, Validation, Holdout, model input,
Candidate ranking, Product activation, canonical writes, or trading.

See [ADR 0296](../decisions/0296-freeze-us-successor-hypothesis-intake-before-source-qualification.md).
