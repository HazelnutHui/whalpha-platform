freezes the minimum evidence path for the three identities excluded by the
first listed-consideration adjudication. It plans two local composite-evidence
reviews and exactly one new official SEC document request.

- the retained official SEC Submissions package and exact Fifth Third member.

and security-language evidence. Neither source alone is silently promoted.
