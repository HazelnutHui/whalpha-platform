# Strong-Leader Pullback Listed-Consideration Source Plan V1

## Purpose

`strong-leader-pullback-listed-consideration-source-plan/1.0` freezes the
smallest official-document plan needed to adjudicate the 12 non-election
listed-equity payoff cases whose cessation timing is already matched.

It is a candidate and source-plan layer. It assigns no consideration-security
identity and calculates no terminal value.

## Inputs and population

The network-disabled planner formally rereads terminal-payoff terms,
source-party relations, cessation decisions, the official SEC Submissions bulk
snapshot, and the point-in-time canonical EOD/Instrument Master bound to each
case's first target-absent exchange session.

The population is exactly the 12 upstream cases in
`listed_security_identity_and_market_value_required`. Holder elections,
unmatched timing, CVRs, and unlisted units are outside this plan and retain
their prior quarantine.

## Candidate evidence

Each plan item retains:

- the exact transaction-document party literal and span;
- one proposed issuer CIK located from that reviewed party;
- the SEC Submissions issuer name, current ticker, exchange, and selected
  transaction registration filing;
- the exact 424B3 accession, filing and acceptance times, primary document,
  and archive URL; and
- one formally read point-in-time common-stock candidate with stable
  `instrument_id`, CIK, FIGI, source identity key, ticker, exchange, EOD
  fingerprint, and Identity fingerprint.

Name and ticker are locators only. Even exact agreement-party, Submissions,
and canonical candidate alignment cannot assign the identity until retained
424B3 content independently proves the same transaction, target security,
consideration share class, and exchange ratio.

The Vivmark case deliberately retains canonical ticker `EQR` at the event
session and SEC current ticker `VMRK`; both point to the same candidate CIK and
stable instrument, but the plan does not promote that observation to a fact.

## Authority

All source-document counts, consideration-security assignments, terminal
values, canonical lifecycle facts, strategy labels, `/data` writes, Historical
Coverage writes, research admissions, Candidate writes, publications,
deployments, and scheduler changes remain zero.

The report is immutable in owner-only `0700/0400` private custody and binds
the implementation, ruleset, all upstream reports, source archive, selected
Submissions members, and canonical evidence sessions.
