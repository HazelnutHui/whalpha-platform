# Strong-Leader Pullback Pre-Research Admission Review V1

`strong-leader-pullback-pre-research-admission-review/1.0` is the final
outcome-blind gate before any real Strong-Leader Pullback research run.

It binds the exact fixed development scope, per-session reconstructed
Membership lineage, action/lifecycle intersection, canonical split action and
adjustment publications, a newly recomputed negative-space split diagnostic,
and the latest terminal-reference census. Family states and exact blockers are
recorded separately; an aggregate percentage cannot hide an incomplete
mandatory family.

The only valid current decision is `rejected_data_blocked`. A rejected report
publishes no Historical Coverage manifest, opens no label, selects no cohort or
parameter, and authorizes no development, Candidate, Production, or scheduler
action. Output is immutable, owner-only, outside `/data`, and exactly
replayable.
