# Strong-Leader Pullback Research Admission V2

`strong-leader-pullback-research-admission/2.1` is an outcome-blind gate for a
clearly labelled latest-vintage reconstructed research lane. It is not a
claim that the historical population was observed live.

Contract 2.1 makes the session denominator explicit: chronological sessions,
feature-window warm-up sessions, sessions with a rankable cross-section, and
complete admitted cross-sections are separate counts. The minimum gate applies
only to the last count. This corrects the unused 2.0 draft's omission of the
20-session feature warm-up from its reconciliation; no prior formal admission
report used 2.0.

The evaluator binds four independently fingerprinted inputs:

- complete-cross-section feature sessions and all pre-outcome exclusions;
- the frozen, naturally completed mechanical split-adjustment evidence;
- exact-reference, interval-reference, and still-unbounded terminal paths; and
- the 24-combination, fixed-cost, chronological and sealed-holdout controls.

Admission requires at least 252 complete feature sessions, complete source
reconciliation for adjustment-only use, a finite interval for every terminal
crossing, a frozen interval policy, and an unconsumed holdout. It does not
permit partial cross-section ranks, point imputation of missing terminal
returns, a complete-case headline, inferred corporate actions, or future
source timestamps.

`ready_for_reconstructed_development` authorizes only development label
construction and development-only parameter selection. Validation, holdout,
performance claims, Candidate activation, canonical writes, network requests,
and Production writes remain false. `blocked` opens nothing and lists the
exact unresolved gates.
