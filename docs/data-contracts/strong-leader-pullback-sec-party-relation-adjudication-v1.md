# Strong-Leader Pullback SEC Party Relation Adjudication V1

## Purpose

`strong-leader-pullback-sec-party-relation-adjudication/1.0` preserves typed
source-party and legal-survivor relations for the 61 first-strategy SEC cases.

It does not assign global stable IDs to counterparties or successors, determine
last tradability, calculate terminal returns, write canonical lifecycle facts,
or admit any case to research or Production.

## Input binding and population

The network-disabled builder rereads and binds the immutable document plan,
source package, cover identity, transaction events, termination reasons, and
common-share consideration report. Raw-document hashes, stable target
`instrument_id` values, accessions, transaction scopes, and every prior
decision fingerprint must agree.

The ruleset is a finite human-reviewed registry for the exact 61 source-hash-
bound cases. It is not a general name extractor and cannot accept a new or
changed case silently.

## Evidence and classification

Every matched decision retains two normalized source substrings with offsets
and SHA-256 digests:

- the agreement-party definition; and
- the completed or consummated merger relation and surviving-entity clause.

The registered topologies are
`target_survives_as_owned_subsidiary`,
`target_absorbed_into_other_survivor`, and
`target_and_peer_absorbed_into_new_holding_company`.

Source role labels and legal names remain evidence, not global identity
assignments. Listed-equity consideration cases are flagged because their
consideration issuer will require a later point-in-time security mapping.

## Authority boundary

Counterparty, successor, and consideration-issuer stable-ID assignment counts
are zero. Canonical party identity, lifecycle fact, terminal outcome,
performance metric, `/data`, Historical Coverage, research admission,
Candidate, publication, deployment, and scheduler authority remain zero.

The canonical JSON report is stored in owner-only `0700/0400` custody and is
bound to code, source, prior evidence, and ruleset fingerprints.
