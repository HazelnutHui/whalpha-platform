# Strong-Leader Pullback SEC Termination Reason Adjudication V1

## Purpose

`strong-leader-pullback-sec-termination-reason-adjudication/1.0` adjudicates
the listing-termination reason for the 61 cases with previously matched SEC
common-equity identity and typed issuer transaction completion.

It does not adjudicate whether or when a stated exchange action became
effective, the first or last tradable session, terminal return, consideration,
party relations, Historical Coverage, research admission, or Production.

## Input binding and scope

The network-disabled builder formally rereads the immutable document plan,
completed source package, cover-identity adjudication, and transaction-event
adjudication. Every raw document, stable ID, accession, and prior decision
fingerprint must agree.

Only a unique bounded Item 3.01 section titled as a delisting notice is
eligible. References such as `Item 3.01, Item 5.01 ...` are not headings and
cannot open a scope.

## Decision rule

`bounded_item_3_01_transaction_listing_link_v1` requires all of:

- a merger, acquisition, or transaction marker;
- a completion, consummation, closing, or explicit causal-link marker; and
- an observed listing or trading action such as a halt, suspension, cessation,
  withdrawal, delisting, no-longer-listed statement, or Form 25 request.

One qualifying section yields `merger_or_acquisition`. Multiple sections are
ambiguous and missing required markers are unsupported. Listing-action words
are retained as evidence categories, not as proof of their effective date.

## Authority boundary

The result resolves the sampled field `termination_reason` at the source-
evidence layer only. It creates no canonical lifecycle or terminal fact and
does not convert a requested, intended, or stated exchange action into an
effective market-status date.

The canonical JSON report is stored in owner-only `0700/0400` custody and is
bound to code, source, prior evidence, and ruleset fingerprints.
