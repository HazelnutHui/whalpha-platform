# Strong-Leader Pullback Terminal-Population Payoff Policy V1

## Purpose

`strong-leader-pullback-terminal-population-payoff-policy/1.0` preserves the
SCS transaction-party relation, normalized election terms, source-stated
default, and bounded future research convention without valuing a payoff or
creating an outcome.

## Inputs and evidence

The report formally binds the corrected source plan and manifest, SEC core
adjudication, and matched daily cessation report. It rereads the exact 8-K,
reconstructs the already-bound introduction and Item 2.01 scope, and verifies
the scope length and SHA-256.

The report retains:

- HNI as the source-local acquirer/consideration-issuer locator and Steelcase
  as the target absorbed into the other legal survivor;
- five exact numeric common-share payoff terms across cash, mixed, and stock
  election alternatives;
- the source sentence assigning the mixed alternative when no valid election
  was made;
- the source calculation reference price, explicitly not as a terminal market
  value; and
- unresolved actual holder election, automatic-adjustment details, and
  proration status.

## Research convention and non-authority

The future daily research convention is
`default_no_valid_election_mixed_with_alternative_sensitivity`: a simulator
that does not submit a holder election uses the contractual no-action default
as its primary reference path and must expose all three alternatives as
sensitivity. It cannot choose the best alternative with hindsight or claim an
actual holder outcome.

No global HNI stable ID, daily listed value, payoff value, terminal outcome,
lifecycle fact, performance result, research admission, `/data`, Historical
Coverage, Candidate, publication, deployment, or scheduler state is created.
The report is immutable in owner-only `0700/0400` custody and exact replay is
idempotent.
