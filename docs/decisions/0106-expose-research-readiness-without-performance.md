# ADR 0106: Expose Research Readiness Without Performance

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-31

## Context

Quant Research Lab has a preregistered first experiment, formal readiness
evidence, chronological fixture mechanics and an adversarially reviewed
statistics plan. It remains `preregistered_data_blocked` at 31 of 252 required
sessions and has no real research result. The product needs a visible home for
this work without converting mechanics, fixtures or coverage into a model
claim.

## Decision

Add Quant Research Lab / 量化研究实验室 as the fifth first-level workspace.
It is available through the same shell to guest and credential Sessions with
no authorization or capability distinction.

The page may show only registered method and formally verified readiness
facts: personal ownership and risk boundary, lifecycle, current `data_blocked`
state, 31/252 history coverage, the Strong-Leader Pullback hypothesis, fixed
24-combination family, chronological split, leakage controls, comparison
group, outcome horizons, advancement gates and missing foundations.

Research readiness must be visually and semantically separate from model
performance. The 31/252 measure is data coverage, never confidence or model
completion. Result panels remain unavailable and may not contain fixture
returns, synthetic charts, win rates or implied performance. Stock outcomes
remain explicitly separate from option outcomes.

The page is static repository product context in this version. It adds no
research API, canonical research adapter, result publication, stage
transition, `/data` write, provider access or Production deployment.

## Consequences

- The research program has an inspectable product home before results exist.
- Users can see what will be tested and what blocks advancement without
  mistaking candidate classification for validated alpha.
- Future real results require formally bound data/result contracts and a
  separate activation decision before this page may display performance.
- Production remains unchanged until a separately authorized deployment.

## Alternatives Considered

### Hide the workspace until 252 sessions exist

Rejected because this conceals the preregistered design and makes later results
harder to distinguish from post-hoc construction.

### Populate the result layout with fixtures

Rejected because synthetic mechanics evidence is not investment evidence.
