# ADR 0265: Stop the First Strategy at a Transitive Data-Admission Gate

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The fixed Strong-Leader Pullback development population has complete price,
stable-identity, and reconstructed daily Membership inputs, plus bounded split
and terminal evidence. Those partial families do not prove omitted-event
neutrality, complete adjustment coverage, or terminal outcomes. Repeating
case-by-case searches without changing source capability would create effort
without changing research authority.

## Decision

1. Produce one immutable, outcome-blind pre-research admission review that
   formally binds the development census, prior rejection, scoped blocker
   census, canonical split action/adjustment evidence, a fresh read-only split
   diagnostic, and terminal-gap census V4.
2. Treat the 287 daily Membership partitions as complete reconstruction input
   for development only. Preserve `as_operated=false` and prohibit their use
   as historical operational snapshots or validation authority.
3. Keep Corporate Action, Adjustment Ledger, Instrument Lifecycle, terminal
   outcomes, and final Historical Coverage unready while their exact blockers
   remain.
4. Do not publish a misleading `research_ready` Historical Coverage manifest.
   The admission report itself records the rejection and acquisition boundary.
5. Stop before opening triggers or outcomes. Resume only after a source can
   provide the measured point-in-time lifecycle/action capabilities, then
   version and rerun the same gate.

## Consequences

The five-year price/identity foundation and daily reconstructed Universe remain
useful and preserved. They are not represented as a completed backtest
database. The next expenditure decision is tied to exact missing capabilities,
not a vendor name or an undirected data purchase.
