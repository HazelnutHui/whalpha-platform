# ADR 0289: Freeze Campaign Three Hypothesis Intake Before Input Qualification

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-16

## Context

The Market-State input passed its frozen qualification and exact replay, but a
qualified input does not define a research question. Campaign Three also
cannot begin from an unrestricted grid of factors, state metrics, thresholds,
and horizons without creating hidden multiple testing.

The first two campaigns consumed 14 formal trials and admitted no candidate
Alpha. Campaign Three is adaptive to that evidence, so proposed ideas must be
identified and deduplicated before implementation or outcomes.

## Decision

1. Register five outcome-blind hypothesis cards and retain both accepted and
   rejected ideas.
2. Advance only three candidate-Alpha interactions and one risk-guard
   interaction to input qualification. This is a prospective budget, not yet
   a registered outcome-trial budget.
3. Reject the ATR-compression plus broad-trend proposal as a near-duplicate of
   the consumed Pullback/ATR-compression family.
4. Bind every card to its exact parent factor, Market-State metric, formula,
   cutoff, Universe, horizon, parameter neighborhood, related family, prior
   trial links, and seven-dimensional duplicate signature.
5. Use a session-level factor-IC interaction estimand. Reject the degenerate
   construction that multiplies a cross-sectional factor by one session-
   constant state scalar and then reranks it.
6. Preserve one primary state axis per mechanism. Do not search all ten state
   metrics, full-sample hindsight thresholds, or undeclared variants.
7. Complete outcome-blind alignment, coverage, timing, variation, episode,
   and replay qualification before registering a screening protocol.
8. Keep Campaign Three, Ledger V4, Development outcomes, Validation, Holdout,
   Model Construction, Product activation, deployment authority, and trading
   authority closed at this decision.

## Consequences

The next engineering work has a finite, reviewable input surface and cannot
silently convert renamed or lightly repackaged old ideas into new evidence.
The rejected fifth idea is retained as negative governance evidence. If one of
the four accepted designs fails outcome-blind input qualification, it stops
without consuming a formal outcome trial.

## Rejected alternatives

- test all ten Market-State metrics against every existing factor;
- fill an arbitrary five-trial quota after deduplication rejected one idea;
- treat the previously qualified risk guard as Alpha;
- use full-panel quantiles to define historical market states; or
- open Development outcomes before input qualification and protocol freeze.
