# ADR 0296: Freeze the U.S. Successor Hypothesis Intake Before Source Qualification

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-17

## Context

Three completed U.S. discovery campaigns consumed 17 formal Development
trials and admitted no Alpha. The independently replayed factor-space
diagnostic then found about 9.14 effective stock-factor dimensions and 4.53
separate Market-State dimensions. It also confirmed dense reversal, path,
leadership, and trailing-risk clusters. Another search over cosmetic OHLCV
windows or state interactions would therefore spend outcome budget on ideas
already represented by the measured library.

## Decision

1. Freeze exactly four new outcome-blind cards: three candidate-Alpha
   mechanisms and one risk guard.
2. The candidate mechanisms are point-in-time FY1 earnings revision yield,
   point-in-time cash earnings quality, and change between two published short
   interest observations. The guard is a scheduled earnings event known by
   the decision cutoff and occurring inside the three-session holding window.
3. Treat these as hypothesis cards only. No formal trial is registered and no
   outcome may be read until its required input has independent point-in-time
   source, timing, coverage, identity, and implementation qualification.
4. Daily short volume cannot substitute for short interest. Current financial
   statements cannot be backfilled as historical knowledge. Current earnings
   calendars cannot stand in for historical event-date revisions.
5. Compare every later proposal against the seven-dimensional duplicate
   identity, all 17 consumed trials, and the measured factor-space clusters.
   Reject renamed windows, PCA-derived Alpha, and further unbounded
   Market-State interactions before outcomes.
6. If fewer than two candidate-Alpha inputs can be qualified without unsafe
   reconstruction, close this intake rather than weakening point-in-time
   requirements.

## Consequences

The next gate is source and implementation qualification, not backtesting.
The intake expands the economic evidence surface beyond trailing price and
volume while preserving a finite search budget. Source cost or unavailability
may close individual cards without consuming a formal outcome trial.

## Rejected alternatives

- another grid of momentum, reversal, volatility, or breadth variants;
- treating PCA components as Alpha;
- substituting daily short volume for reported short interest;
- using restated financials without as-filed publication times; or
- registering Development trials before point-in-time input qualification.
