# ADR 0298: Close the U.S. Successor Intake at Source Qualification

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-17

## Context

ADR 0296 froze three candidate-Alpha cards and one event-risk guard without
outcomes. The next gate asked whether their exact point-in-time meanings can be
implemented from verified local sources. It also required closure rather than
weaker semantics if fewer than two candidate-Alpha inputs qualify.

## Decision

1. Close the intake as `closed_source_blocked`: zero of three Alpha inputs and
   zero of one risk guards currently qualify.
2. FY1 revision yield has no historical consensus-vintage source. Published
   short-interest change has neither issue-level publication history nor a
   point-in-time security-level shares denominator. The earnings guard has no
   historical event announcement and revision ledger.
3. Cash earnings quality may reuse the SEC as-filed foundation, but the exact
   CFO/NI/Assets TTM derivation, amendment handling, listed-security projection,
   nonfinancial applicability, and joint coverage have not been registered or
   qualified. Raw concept presence is not feature coverage.
4. Register zero formal trials and read zero outcomes. Do not replace any card
   with price, daily short-volume, current-restatement, current-calendar, or
   ticker-broadcast proxies.
5. Correct the short-interest card's lineage before freezing this report:
   dollar-volume surprise is a related setup input, not a consumed formal
   outcome trial.
6. A later attempt requires newly acquired or explicitly authorized
   point-in-time sources and a new intake/report version. It cannot rewrite
   this closure.

## Consequences

The program remains capable of future research without manufacturing a trial
from unavailable data. SEC cash-quality engineering may proceed as a separate
outcome-blind source project, while consensus, short-interest, and event-
calendar cards wait for appropriate sources. Model Construction remains locked.
