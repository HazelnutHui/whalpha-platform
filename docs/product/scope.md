# Product Scope

## Current product

- Market Regime & Opportunities;
- Sector ETF Rotation;
- Market Structure & Activity;
- bilingual guest and credential Sessions with identical capability;
- a deployed but unvalidated Candidate/Strategy Channels baseline; and
- a research-only Quant Research Lab with no performance claim.

## Active development scope

- use the implemented Quant Research Lab registry as the transparent
  methodology, validation, failure, and lifecycle authority;
- complete only the data families required by a named research question;
- research one falsifiable strategy family at a time, beginning with
  Strong-Leader Pullback;
- prove one complete, rejection-capable research path before generalizing it
  into a bounded AI Quant Research Factory;
- publish professional research metrics on a slower reviewed cadence;
- activate at most a small number of validated models into Stock Candidates;
  and
- later redesign Candidate presentation around the exact activated model,
  current-market applicability, entry readiness, and invalidation.

The current heuristic Candidate score and three technical Strategy Channels
remain visible Production facts but are frozen as unvalidated `Baseline V1`.
They are not the target model architecture and must not be tuned directly.

## Later scope

- specialized hypothesis, data, implementation, statistics, cost, red-team,
  reproduction, and shadow-monitor agents under finite experiment budgets;
- Momentum Breakout, distinct Trend Continuation, Technical Reversal, and
  Fundamental Value Reversal research;
- governed sector/industry classification and concentration diagnostics;
- fundamentals, relative valuation, earnings, guidance, macro, and news as
  point-in-time evidence;
- an options-expression layer with chain liquidity, IV, Greeks, events, payoff,
  strike, structure, and DTE evidence;
- portfolio/position risk and IBKR integration; and
- intraday research only after a distinct use case and dataset justify it.

## Explicitly outside current scope

- automated execution or order routing;
- HFT;
- naked short-option recommendations;
- opaque or ungoverned ML/prediction systems;
- large microservice, Kubernetes, or distributed-system architecture without
  demonstrated need; and
- guest restrictions before the equal-capability product policy is explicitly
  changed.
