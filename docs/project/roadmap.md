# Roadmap

This is future sequencing, not operational authorization. Verified facts belong
in [current context](current-context.md), actual capability in
[current status](current-status.md), and completed detail in the
[changelog](changelog.md), ADRs, and audits.

## Direction

Quant Research Lab is the research and evidence center. The durable sequence is:

```text
governed point-in-time data
-> Factor Discovery
-> Model Construction
-> Strategy Expression
-> locked Validation and one sealed Holdout
-> prospective shadow
-> separate activation
-> Stock Candidates
-> monitoring, decay, and retirement
```

Research is not limited to a fixed queue of Pullback, Breakout, Continuation,
or other named models. Those labels remain possible hypothesis territories.
Each outcome-reading campaign is finite and trial-counted, while successive
campaigns may expand the long-term factor and model universe.

The current Candidate score and six Strategy Channels remain frozen,
unvalidated Baseline V1 compatibility behavior. They are not the future
research architecture.

## Working rules

- Register the mechanism, data, role, trial budget, and stopping rule before
  reading the relevant outcomes.
- Qualify factor coverage and redundancy without outcomes before screening.
- Use a small admitted factor set and the simplest defensible model baseline.
- Keep factor, model, and strategy-expression versions independently traceable.
- Lock the full lineage before Validation; open Holdout once only.
- Keep all attempts and failures in the true multiplicity ledger.
- Keep the overall discovery program renewable while making every
  outcome-reading campaign finite, deduplicated, preregistered, replayed, and
  closed before the next campaign reads outcomes.
- Separate event studies from portfolios and stock outcomes from option
  outcomes.
- Add or buy data only for a named feature, label, bias control, execution
  assumption, or decision.
- Stop at each phase exit criterion; do not keep completed execution narrative
  in this roadmap.

## Completed research baseline

Strong-Leader Pullback completed one reproducible, rejection-capable
development path. V1 ended `inconclusive_evidence_floor`; its single registered
replacement ended `rejected_endpoint_instability`. No parameter was locked,
Validation and Holdout were not opened, and no performance or Candidate
authority exists. Detailed evidence remains in the final audit and archive.

This result is not retuned. Its reusable lesson is the governed rejection path,
not its named setup.

## Current gated phase — Successor outcome-blind intake

No campaign is active. The next cycle may begin only with a new finite set of
falsifiable hypotheses deduplicated against all 17 consumed trials. Research
territories may expand, but Campaign Three formulas and thresholds cannot be
retuned after the result.

Sequence:

1. propose economically motivated hypotheses without reading new outcomes;
2. apply the seven-dimensional duplicate and related-family review;
3. name required point-in-time inputs and reject ideas the current data cannot
   support honestly;
4. freeze a finite qualification budget before implementation;
5. qualify inputs without outcomes and independently replay the result;
6. preregister only qualified trials in the next cumulative ledger edition;
7. require a new clean implementation, exact authorization, formal run, and
   exact replay before any further Development result is read.

Exit: a separately registered successor campaign, or a retained no-launch
decision. This phase grants no Development, Validation, Holdout, model,
Candidate, publication, broker, or trading authority.

## Conditional next phase — Model Construction

Only admitted factor evidence can open this phase.

1. retain every attempted factor and interaction in the trial ledger;
2. screen only the registered development partition;
3. choose a small non-redundant set or choose none;
4. compare transparent rank/rule and linear baselines first;
5. admit additional complexity only for stable incremental value;
6. freeze target, benchmark, formula/algorithm, features, parameters,
   calibration, applicability, uncertainty, and failure conditions; and
7. independently reproduce the locked model package.

Exit: one locked model eligible for strategy-expression research, or a
retained rejection. A model still has no Candidate authority.

## Next gated phase — Strategy Expression

Translate a locked model into a separately versioned decision system:

1. freeze Universe, cutoff, entry/wait/skip/invalidation/exit, holding,
   cooldown, and re-entry rules;
2. freeze concurrency, sizing, cash, risk, concentration, turnover, liquidity,
   costs, capacity, and failure behavior;
3. keep stock and option vehicles in separate evidence systems;
4. compare only the finite registered expression alternatives in development;
5. lock model plus expression before Validation; and
6. permit portfolio metrics only when the portfolio is fully specified.

Exit: one complete locked factor-model-expression lineage or a retained
rejection.

## Evaluation and Product path

1. execute locked chronological Validation;
2. stop on failure without tuning;
3. consume one sealed Holdout only after Validation passes;
4. independently reproduce and red-team the result;
5. run prospectively in shadow/paper state;
6. conduct a separate activation review; and
7. redesign Stock Candidates around the exact active lineage, within-model
   rank/estimate, factor contributions, current applicability, entry readiness,
   counterevidence, invalidation, freshness, and monitoring.

The active Product set remains intentionally small through a reviewed display
and operational-risk budget, not a permanent numeric model cap.

## Then — Bounded AI Quant Research Factory

Only after one full three-layer path survives evaluation and shadow:

1. extract reusable factor, model, expression, experiment, result, and failure
   registries;
2. deduplicate related ideas and retain true trial counts;
3. isolate development, Validation, Holdout, red-team, reproduction, and
   shadow access;
4. expand the small outcome-blind multi-agent pilot beyond its current
   manually supervised scope only after measured evidence justifies it;
5. measure rejection quality, leakage detection, reproducibility, compute
   cost, and Holdout integrity before scaling; and
6. keep activation, capital, broker access, and order execution outside agent
   authority.

## Later evidence expansion

- market, sector, industry, cross-asset, and defensive-state factors;
- point-in-time fundamentals, valuation, earnings, guidance, and event factors;
- observed spread/impact and capacity calibration;
- historical option chain, IV, skew, Greeks, events, adjustments, and
  independent option expressions;
- multi-model portfolio research, position management, account risk, and later
  IBKR integration; and
- intraday data only for a registered use case that justifies its cost and
  complexity.

These are input and research territories, not a fixed strategy queue.

## Parallel product and operations work

- Keep the Lab website synchronized with real factor/model/expression state and
  never market a research-only record as validated.
- Keep the three market-context workspaces stable and fast.
- Keep daily operation bounded and fail-closed; reopen optimization only after
  a measured budget breach.
- Preserve guest/credential equality until explicitly changed.

## Explicitly deferred

- automated trading, order routing, and HFT;
- unbounded factor/model search or opaque prediction;
- Validation or Holdout access before a locked lineage;
- guest/credential capability differences;
- new databases, microservices, Kubernetes, or distributed compute without a
  measured need; and
- paid data without a named requirement and acceptance test.
