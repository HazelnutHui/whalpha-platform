## Send-ready message

Hello Massive Support,

I use Massive Stocks data for a personal, non-commercial U.S. equity research
tool called WH Alpha. I would appreciate written clarification on the plan or
license required for the exact use below.

The application runs on my Dell workstation, where data is acquired, retained,
normalized, and used for derived analytics and backtests. A Session-protected
website displays the same data and functions to me and a small group of friends;
guest and credential Sessions are intentionally identical. The tool supports
discretionary research only. It does not execute orders, resell a raw feed, or
operate commercially.

Please confirm:

1. Which plan, add-on, or agreement permits this use, including access by
   friends through the Session-protected website?
2. Whether I may acquire and retain raw responses, normalized historical facts,
   corrections, and derived research on my Dell workstation, and any retention
   or deletion requirements during or after subscription termination.
3. Whether I may compute indicators, rankings, charts, backtests, and model
   diagnostics, then display the derived results identically to guest and
   credential Sessions and deliver the necessary JSON to their browsers.
4. Whether raw values such as close, volume, split, and dividend facts may also
   be displayed identically, and what attribution, exchange, user-count,
   reporting, or audit requirements apply.
5. Whether my current account is entitled to approximately one year of
   historical access through Grouped Daily with `adjusted=false`, point-in-time
   All Tickers with both `active=true` and `active=false`, Splits, and Dividends.
6. The exact earliest accessible date, rate/pagination limits, stable daily
   completion time, correction/versioning process, and coverage limitations for
   inactive or delisted securities.
7. Whether merger, spinoff, successor-security, ticker-change, last-tradable-
   session, delisting-reason, and terminal-outcome evidence is available, and
   through which endpoints or products.
8. All one-time, recurring, redistribution/display, or exchange pass-through
   fees for the exact use described above.

For engineering purposes, I need each of these uses answered explicitly:
workstation acquisition, raw retention, derived analysis, equal guest/login raw
display, equal guest/login derived display, and browser/API delivery. If any use
requires a separate agreement, please identify it rather than assuming it is
included in the standard individual plan.

Thank you.
