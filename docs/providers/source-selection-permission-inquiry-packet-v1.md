## Questions every commercial candidate must answer in writing

1. What exact legal entity, product, plan, exchanges, endpoints, and account
   entitlement would cover the use below?
2. May Dell acquire and retain raw responses? State any maximum retention.
3. May WH Alpha retain normalized canonical facts and corrections indefinitely?
4. May Dell calculate internal derived panels, indicators, scores, ranks,
   backtests, and model diagnostics?
5. May raw values such as close, volume, split, and dividend facts be displayed
   identically to guest and credential Sessions used by the owner and friends?
6. May derived charts, scores, classifications, explanations, and alerts be
   displayed identically to those Sessions?
7. May the required raw and derived values be delivered as JSON to a web
   browser through a Session-protected application?
8. Does non-commercial access by friends count as display, redistribution, a
   derived work, an application, or another licensed use?
9. What attribution, exchange agreement, user count, device, audit, reporting,
   or display requirements apply?
10. On plan change or termination, what must be deleted: raw responses,
    normalized facts, canonical history, derived panels, displayed artifacts,
    research results, or backups? State the deadline and any survival rights.
11. What historical depth is available for active, inactive, and delisted U.S.
    securities, and how is point-in-time symbol/reference history exposed?
12. Are bars unadjusted, split-adjusted, dividend-adjusted, or total-return
    adjusted? Are separate action records and exact revision timestamps exposed?
13. How are late corrections, cancellations, restatements, duplicate events,
    and historical replays versioned and announced?
14. What are the earliest stable daily completion time, service-level target,
    rate limits, pagination rules, retry guidance, and batch/request limits?
15. Which exchanges and action types are absent, delayed, estimated, or
    supplied by third parties?
16. For mergers, spinoffs, and delistings, are successor/security IDs,
    consideration, effective date, last tradable session, and terminal reason
    available?
17. What one-time and recurring fees apply to the exact use, including
    redistribution/display add-ons and exchange pass-through fees?
18. For classification data, which stable company and security identifiers are
    delivered, and what licensed crosswalk resolves them to each listed share
    class without relying on ticker or name?
19. Which fields separately represent business-effective dates, first
    availability, provider observation/update time, revision, cancellation,
    and taxonomy-version changes? Does a historical extract reproduce what was
    knowable then or apply later corrections backward?
20. May classification codes/names and derived sector or industry counts,
    concentration diagnostics, charts, filters, and explanations be displayed
    identically to guest and credential Sessions through browser JSON?
